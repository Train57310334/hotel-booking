# Welcome to BookingKub

This guide will help you set up your hotel and get ready for your first booking in 5 easy steps.

### 1. Hotel Setup
Navigate to **Settings** to configure your hotel name, address, and upload your logo.

### 2. Create Room Types
Go to **Rooms > Room Types** to create your base categories (e.g., Deluxe, Standard).

### 3. Add Physical Rooms
Under **Rooms**, add the actual room numbers associated with your Room Types.

### 4. Setup Rate Plans
Define your pricing in **Rates**. You can create standard rates, non-refundable rates, or seasonal rates.

### 5. Configure Payments
In **Settings**, enter your payment gateway keys (Stripe or Omise) to accept online bookings.
